The BarHouse Story

Meets Design

Since 2012, BarHouse has redefined the home cocktail experience — blending premium barware, custom furniture design, and professional mixology tools to transform ordinary spaces into extraordinary home bars.

Our Philosophy

Beyond the Tool:
The Art of the Home Bar

BarHouse was founded on a simple truth: a cocktail is more than a drink — it's a moment, a memory, a crafted emotion. We bring the elegance of a high-end cocktail lounge directly to your home, offering everything from bespoke bar cabinets to professional-grade shakers and crystal glassware.

Every piece in our collection is curated by industry professionals. We don't just sell tools — we elevate your craft. From sleek jiggers to complete custom bar installations, BarHouse is the heart of your home entertaining.
